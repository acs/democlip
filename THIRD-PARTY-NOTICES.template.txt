DemoClip itself is MIT licensed (see LICENSE.md). The built app bundles the

This is the template: the build fills in the version from what is actually
installed (see the `democlip-notices` plugin in vite.config.ts) and ships the

  MIT License
  Copyright (c) 2023-2025 Bluesky Social PBC  ·  core-js © 2014-2026 Denis Pushkarev

  What it does here: the optional Bluesky sign-in and publishing path. It drives
  a public-client OAuth flow — PKCE, pushed authorization requests, and DPoP —
  and signs the account's own requests. It is loaded only when a clip is
  published, never on a normal load, and never at all if you do not publish.
