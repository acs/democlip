    Publish to Bluesky

    Sign in with your handle — your server asks you to approve, then brings you back here. The page reloads on the way back, and your recording comes back with it; you only do this once.

    Bluesky takes up to 10 minutes and 300 MB, and re-encodes at about 1920 wide — a wider screen is scaled down, and small text with it. Record a region (the red box) around the window you are filming and its text stays full size.

    Upload to YouTube

    A default YouTube account takes videos up to 15 minutes. The video is on your channel as soon as it is uploaded, and YouTube finishes processing it just after — so it may take a moment to play. The app is not allowed to read your channel, so it cannot watch that part.
