1
Choose what to record
Nothing is captured until you turn a source on. Press Screen to pick a screen, window or tab (full screen is preselected) with or without audio if available. Webcam gives a camera bubble over the screen — or the whole picture on its own when the screen is off. Mic adds your voice: its level shows in the little meter and is mixed with the tab audio, and on its own it records a sound-only clip, with no picture. Press Import to bring a WebM or MP4 file — a video, or a sound file — already on your machine.
2
Hit record
Press the red button to start recording. Press again to stop. Press it once more after a recording and the new take is added to the end of the clip, so you can join several recordings. In the Timeline you will see the pictures and sound as they are captured.
3
Stop and cut
Once stopped, press ▶ (or P) to play the clip back. Click the timeline to land on an exact moment; mark where a part starts and ends and press the scissors to cut it out — the timeline closes up over the gap. Press the arrow, image, text, sound or magnifier beside the clip's pencil to point at something over a stretch instead, and its settings set how long it is shown — a sound plays one of the app's own sounds over it, mixed with the recording, and you pick which one from a drop-down in its settings, with one play button to hear the chosen sound; a magnifier is dragged from the detail outward, shows that detail enlarged inside a round lens and dims the rest, and its settings change the rim's colour and the zoom.
4
Export and publish
Give the clip a title and a description in the settings control, and set its Export resolution beside them — the size the file is finally written at, a smaller one for a smaller file — then press Export to download a WebM or MP4 file — or publish it to Bluesky or YouTube after signing in. A sound-only clip exports the sound alone, and a video service is not offered for it. Either file is encoded faster than real time, with the timeline counting down what is left.
